Five questions worth asking

  • Is the requested work complete?
  • Do the facts have support?
  • Were the constraints preserved?
  • Did corrections reach dependent claims?
  • Is the next step clear?

Completion is not accuracy

A complete checklist records your review. It does not establish that every statement is true.
